Atrium HR: attendance, leave and payroll in one place

Atrium is a demo HR workspace. Sign in above to open the admin console or the employee mobile app and try employees, attendance, leave, payroll, hiring and reports with sample data.

What you can try

  • Employees: Profiles, departments, employment types and documents in one directory.
  • Attendance: Daily attendance, a monthly muster and employee check-in with a selfie and geofence.
  • Leave: Leave types, balances, requests and approvals with a clear policy.
  • Payroll: Monthly pay runs with basic pay, HRA, PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS, plus payslips.
  • Hiring: Job openings, a candidate pipeline, interviews with feedback, and offers with onboarding.
  • Reports: Attendance trends, headcount by department, hiring funnel and cost charts.

Admin console and employee app

HR admins manage people, approve leave, run payroll and read reports in the console. Employees use a phone-sized app to check in with a selfie inside the office geofence, apply for leave and view payslips.

About the demo data

Everything runs in your browser and uses sample data. Changes stay on your device. Do not enter real employee or payroll information.
